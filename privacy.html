FlightPal Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Overview

FlightPal is designed to minimise the collection of personal information. FlightPal does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics or user tracking.

Location

When you allow location access, FlightPal uses your location to show nearby aerodromes and your position on the map.

FlightPal does not send your precise location to FlightPal-operated servers.

Information Stored on Your Device

FlightPal stores certain information locally on your device so that the app can remember your preferences and provide its features.

This may include:

  • Aircraft profiles
  • Aircraft registrations
  • Advisory crosswind and gust thresholds
  • Favourite aerodromes
  • Unit preferences
  • Map and nearby radius settings
  • Selected departure and destination aerodromes
  • Whiteboard drawings

External Services

FlightPal requests aviation information from external services in order to provide weather, NOTAM and official publication information.

These services include:

  • AviationWeather.gov for METAR and TAF information
  • NATS services for NOTAM and UK AIP/publication information
  • Apple MapKit and Core Location for map and location functionality

FlightPal generally requests aviation information using aerodrome identifiers rather than your precise location.

As with normal internet requests, external service providers may receive standard network information such as your IP address.

Tracking and Advertising

FlightPal does not sell personal information and does not use personal information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Data Retention

FlightPal stores app preferences and user-created information locally on your device.

Removing FlightPal from your device will normally remove locally stored FlightPal data.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if FlightPal's features, data sources or data-handling practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, please use the contact information on the FlightPal Support page.
