About FlightPal

FlightPal is a UK general aviation reference and briefing aid providing access to nearby aerodromes, aviation weather, NOTAMs, official publications, route briefing tools and runway wind information.

Aviation Disclaimer

FlightPal is a general aviation reference and briefing aid. It is not an approved navigation system, certified flight-planning system, or substitute for an official pre-flight briefing.

Weather, NOTAM, aerodrome, runway wind and publication information may be delayed, incomplete, unavailable or superseded.

Always verify operational information using current official sources and applicable aircraft documentation before flight.
